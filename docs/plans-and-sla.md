# Plans and uptime

## Starter

Starter is £49 per month. The uptime target is 99.9% in a calendar month. Support is email on business days, 09:00–17:30 UK time. Starter does not include a named contact.

## Growth

Growth is £149 per month. The uptime target is 99.9% in a calendar month. Support is email and chat on business days, 09:00–17:30 UK time.

## Scale

Scale is £499 per month. The uptime target is 99.95% in a calendar month. Support is email and chat, 08:00–20:00 UK time on business days, plus a named contact. No Northline plan includes a 99.99% uptime target. Do not promise 99.99% on any plan.

## Service credits

If a month misses the target for that customer's plan, they can request a service credit of 10% of that month's fee. The request must arrive within 30 days of the month ending. Credits are not applied automatically.
