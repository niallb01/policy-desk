# Billing

## Invoices

Subscription invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. The invoice email is the billing contact on the account, not the person who filed the ticket, unless they are the same.

## Failed payments

A failed payment has a 7-day grace period. During those 7 days the product keeps working and billing emails the customer. After day 7 the account becomes read-only. After day 14 of non-payment the account is suspended. An agent tells the customer those two dates. An agent does not extend them.

## Double charges

If two successful charges exist for the same invoice, refund the duplicate to the original payment method. Do not ask the customer to raise a bank dispute. Note the invoice id and both charge ids on the ticket.
