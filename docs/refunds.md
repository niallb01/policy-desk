# Refunds

Northline refunds are decided from this page. Agents quote this page. They do not offer a refund this page does not allow.

## Annual plans

An annual plan can be refunded in full when the request arrives within 30 days of the charge. The charge is either the first purchase or a renewal. After day 30 the annual charge is not refunded. The customer can still turn off auto-renew so the next year is not billed.

## Monthly plans

A monthly charge can be refunded in full when the request arrives within 14 days of that charge. After day 14 that charge is not refunded. Cancelling stops the next month. It does not return the current month.

## How the money is returned

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. They appear within 10 business days. Agents cannot approve an exception. A billing lead can approve an exception, and the ticket must record their name.
